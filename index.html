About this encyclopedia

An interactive map of the Spider-Verse and the wider Marvel Cinematic Universe: 60+ characters across the MCU, the Raimi and Webb Spider-Man films, and the animated Spider-Verse, connected by a living relationship graph. Explore the map, browse the encyclopedia, and track the MCU in the order Marvel released it.

Threat Level is an app-created 1–10 classification for exploring the dataset — it is not an official Marvel ranking. 1–2 Minimal · 3–4 Low · 5–6 Moderate · 7–8 High · 9 Extreme · 10 Catastrophic.
Imagery. Character images are pulled live from Marvel Fandom / Wikipedia so the encyclopedia uses real character artwork or film imagery rather than generated placeholder portraits. Actors and films use their Wikipedia / Wikimedia images. Anything without a match falls back to a styled initials avatar so nothing appears broken.
Music. Click Music in the top bar to choose an audio file from your computer to play as the score (it stays on your device). With no file chosen, the app plays an original heroic fanfare synthesized live in your browser.
Data accuracy. Actors, films, release dates, powers, relationships and suit histories are drawn from the theatrical films. Alternate versions of the same character (e.g. the three live-action Peter Parkers) are kept as separate entities, as they are separate people across separate universes.

Built as an interactive exploration tool. Drag nodes, zoom the map, click anything.
